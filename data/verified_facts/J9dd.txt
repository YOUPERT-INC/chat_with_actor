Sakura Mana (紗倉まな) is also a novelist. Two books:

1. 「최저。」 (最低。, "Saitei.") - her debut novel. Published by KADOKAWA / Media Factory in February 2016 (single volume) and as a Kadokawa Bunko paperback in September 2017. Filmed in autumn 2017 (director Seiji Sese / 瀬々敬久), shown in the competition section of the Tokyo International Film Festival.
   Official blurb: a linked short-story collection about four women around the AV industry: Ayako (아야코), a girl who hates her mother because of her mother's AV past; Ayano (아야노, 彩乃), a popular AV actress who keeps working without telling her family; Momoko (모모코, 桃子), a woman from Susukino who moved to Tokyo with the man she loved; Miho (미호, 美穂), a housewife who decided to appear after seeing her husband's AV.
   Reader summary (secondary source, may contain small errors; stay at this level): Ayano left the shadow of her family for Tokyo and was scouted into becoming a popular AV actress. Miho lost confidence when her husband slept apart and turned to AV; she stepped in front of the camera herself to feel again that she was attractive as a woman. Ayako is teased in a small town over the rumour about her mother and wanders between anger and complicated love for her mother and grandmother. The four are not tied up neatly; the note it ends on is that each of them, hurt as they are, can still take one step forward.

2. 「요철」 (凹凸, "Outotsu") - her first full-length novel, published by KADOKAWA in March 2017.
   Official blurb: in the 13th year of their marriage their long-awaited first child, Shiori, is born, and from that day the couple ends their relationship as man and woman. Kinuko (기누코, 絹子), who chooses to part from her husband Masayuki (마사유키, 正幸), tries stubbornly to live only as a mother in order to protect Shiori (시오리, 栞).
   Reader summary (secondary source, may contain small errors; stay at this level): told in turn from the mother's, father's and daughter's points of view. Kinuko married the much older Masayuki in a hurry to escape her own hard family, then gave up her life as a woman to be only a mother. Masayuki refused the role of husband and father, followed his own desires, had affairs and left. The grown-up Shiori, who was hurt when her father left, finds his traces in her older boyfriend and clings to him. The title's concave-and-convex image is about people forcing themselves to fit each other's gaps; the wounds are not healed neatly, and the book is about the painful growing up of facing your family and other people.
